The Executive Journey

I am Orifha Joan Thompson, a technology and business professional building at the multi-disciplinary intersection of technology, business, and human capability.

My journey began in Banking and Finance, establishing a rigorous foundation in operational risk, financial stewardship, and corporate strategy. I subsequently pivoted into Cybersecurity Operations & Threat Management to master technical defense.

Today, I am expanding that cross-functional capability through a Master’s degree in Business Analytics and Applied AI. This synthesis allows me to evaluate technology investments not as isolated IT projects, but as strategic drivers of commercial enterprise growth.
